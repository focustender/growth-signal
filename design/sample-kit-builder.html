Why this is the intervention point, not a generic redesign

  1. It targets the specific gap, not the whole funnel. Trade-show leads already convert reasonably once they request a second sample or upload a project — the drop happens strictly between "one sample" and "anything after." The module only appears in that exact state, so it doesn't add friction for accounts already progressing normally.
  2. It reduces the decision, it doesn't just remind. Rather than a generic "still interested?" nudge, it surfaces the specific companion tile and grout combination most often specified alongside the customer's actual first pick — sourced from real specification co-occurrence, not a generic upsell.
  3. It has an explicit exit ramp. "Skip, start a project instead" is given equal visual weight to the kit suggestion — for accounts that don't need a second sample and are ready to scope a project, the module doesn't force an unnecessary step.
  4. It's measurable on arrival. Second-sample-request rate and project-upload rate for prompted vs. unprompted trade-show accounts is the direct read, matching experiment #2 in the measurement foundation.
